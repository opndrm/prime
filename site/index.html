macOS 14+ required

OPNDRM VM

A clean macOS app, like UTM, but for AI control. Create VMs, connect any AI agent, and watch it work in a safe sandbox.

curl -fsSL https://opndrm.com/install | bash

VM Management

Create, save, restore, clone, and destroy VMs. Apple or Linux. One at a time or four in a quad view.

AI-agnostic

Buzz, Codex, OpenCode, Prime Agent, or any future agent. Connects via a simple socket. One agent per VM.

Recording

Record VM sessions with OpenAdapt. Replay them. Feed recordings to AI agents as training material.

Open Dream Workflow

One command installs WezTerm, HERDR, Prime Agent, JCode, Ollama, oMLX, and Handy inside any VM.

Disk Management

Visual disk usage. Per-VM breakdown. Snapshot cleanup. Know exactly how much space you have.

Safe Sandbox

AI works inside the VM. Nothing leaks to your desktop. The host is just a remote control.
